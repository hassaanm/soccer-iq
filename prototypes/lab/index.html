Lab: new ways to play

Three tries at a new match. Each one plays for 3 minutes, then shows its counts. The letter and the small stamp in the corner (for example "Lab A · v1") name what you saw, for feedback.

A Your ball, always

He plays whoever on his team has the ball, so every touch is his choice and play never stops. Watch for: does he stay with it with no breaks, and pick the open friend?

 B Get open first

He is one player and makes most choices without the ball: where to run so a friend can pass, and where to stand when they have it. Watch for: does he find the open spot on his own?

 C Keep the ball

Four keep it from two in a box. The picture never stops, so who is open keeps changing. Watch for: does he look again before he taps, or tap the first friend he sees?
