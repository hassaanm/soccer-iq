Lab: new ways to play

One match, seen from his own player, in four flows. He picks his player at the start (the shirt at the top right changes him). Each plays 3 minutes, then shows its counts. The stamp in the corner ("Lab D · v1") names what you saw. Nothing here changes his progress or stickers.

D Step by step

Play slows right down at every choice, with the ball and without it, and he taps a friend, the goal or a spot. Watch for: does he look round before he picks?

 E Flowing

One calm speed, never stops; he acts when he wants with taps (run, pass, shoot) and drags to look. Watch for: does he act on his own, or wait for the hint?

 H Hold to think

Flows a little quicker, but a finger held on the pitch slows the world while he looks. Watch for: does he learn to hold and look when the ball comes?

 I Coach asks

Flows freely; now and then the coach stops it with one question ("who is open?", "where is the space?", "who goes?") he answers on the pitch. Watch for: are the questions too often, too rare?

Earlier tries (top view)

A Your ball, always

He plays whoever on his team has the ball, so every touch is his choice and play never stops. Watch for: does he stay with it with no breaks, and pick the open friend?

 B Get open first

He is one player and makes most choices without the ball: where to run so a friend can pass, and where to stand when they have it. Watch for: does he find the open spot on his own?

 C Keep the ball

Four keep it from two in a box. The picture never stops, so who is open keeps changing. Watch for: does he look again before he taps, or tap the first friend he sees?
