# Dribble vs. pass for ages 5–8

Summary of the evidence behind the v21 rebalance. No source gives a numeric split; the targets below are inferred.

## What the sources say

- **The FA (England DNA foundation phase, Pete Sturgess):** encourage U5–U8 players to stay on the ball. Telling kids to "pass early to the easiest option" blocks their development. Stay on the ball when there's no positive pass, there's space to run into, it's a 1v1, or every pass goes sideways or back. https://thefa.com/-/media/cfa/northridingfa/files/news/foundation-phase-notes.ashx
- **Belgium (Voetbal Vlaanderen 2v2 brochure):** U6 is the "me and the ball" stage, so passing is of minor importance. The age order is running with the ball and shooting, then 1v1, then passing from U7. https://belgianfootball.s3.eu-central-1.amazonaws.com/s3fs-public/voetbalvlaanderen/Competitie/Veldvoetbal/PDF/Brochure+-+2v2+Dribbelvoetbal.pdf
- **KNVB:** in the U7 4v4 format, restarts are dribbled in, and the goal is many touches and lots of dribbling and scoring. https://www.knvb.nl/ontdek-voetbal/pupillenvoetbal/onder-7
- **DFB (Kinderfußball reform, Funino):** the formats are 2v2 and 3v3 on four minigoals, with as many ball actions as possible. Kids learn dribbling first, and passing comes in as playing together with a partner. https://assets.dfb.de/uploads/000/236/239/original_Booklet_-_Spielformen_im_Kinderfußball.pdf
- **Research:**
  - Smaller games give more dribbles, passes and shots per child: Lex et al. 2022 (doi:10.1007/s12662-021-00748-0) and Bergmann et al. 2022 (doi:10.1177/17479541211051654).
  - Egocentric play is normal up to about ages 8–10 (De la Vega 2002, cited in Spanish studies).

## Targets used in the game

- **Early lessons** (look up, run into space, take him on, shoot): about 80/20 dribble or shoot vs. pass.
- **Later lessons** (two shirts, pass to the open friend, forward first): more passing, about 65/35 by ages 7–8.
- **Passing cues** to keep, one per scene: two defenders closing in, a teammate clearly free when your path is blocked, and a teammate clearly closer to goal.
- **Never right:** a pass back just to keep the ball, or "pass early" when there's space ahead. A sideways or back pass when there's space ahead earns 2★ (safe).
